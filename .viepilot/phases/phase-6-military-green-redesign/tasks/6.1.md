# Task 6.1: Update Global Themes to Military Green

## Objective
Apply a 100% bright, military green theme (white text, green background) across the entire application, abandoning the previous dark theme.

## Paths
- `server/ui_components.py`
- `server/main_app.py`

## Implementation Plan
